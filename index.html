I am a fourth-year PhD student in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington, advised by Thomas Trogdon. My research lies at the intersection of random matrix theory, orthogonal polynomials, and numerical linear algebra. I am particularly interested in the behavior of Krylov methods on large random matrices, with applications to spectral density estimation, spike detection, and covariance estimation.
